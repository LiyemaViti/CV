# Evidence checklist for the final portfolio

The rubric awards 10 marks for evidence and 10 for reflection in each of five areas. Aim to make each area easy to verify.

| Rubric area | Evidence to include | Reflection to include | Done? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Agile Team Charter | Actual charter, roles/agreements, supporting screenshot | What worked, a real challenge, improvement | [ ] |
| User Stories and Tasks | User stories, acceptance criteria, task breakdown, board/commit links | How requirements were translated into work | [ ] |
| Product Backlog | Actual or clearly labelled reconstructed prioritised backlog | How priorities were chosen and changed | [ ] |
| Sprint Backlog | Actual sprint goal, dates, owners, tasks and statuses | What happened in the sprint and what was learned | [ ] |
| Agile Logbook | Genuine dated notes or traceable project activity | Learning, blockers and improvement | [ ] |
